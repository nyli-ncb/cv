Where I am taking the PMO

Enterprise portfolio management is moving away from the administrative PMO. This is the direction I am taking the practice in, and the standard I would set in a new mandate.

01

Strategic portfolio management, not project administration

Governance should answer “is this still the right investment?” as often as “is it on schedule?” The PMO's value sits in connecting strategy to execution across a mixed agile and waterfall estate.

02

Reporting as decision intelligence

Replace the periodic status pack with what actually changed, why it matters and which decision it forces. Executives should receive exceptions and trade-offs, not a data dump.

03

AI-assisted portfolio operations, human accountability

Let AI carry the administrative load — status assembly, data reconciliation, portfolio hygiene — so PMO capacity shifts from maintaining visibility to interpreting it. Named owners stay accountable for every call.

04

Risk as a live signal, not a static log

Read drift from delivery data before it reaches a formal escalation, so escalation becomes a decision point rather than an autopsy.

05

Continuous capacity and funding

Move from annual lock-in and periodic reforecasting to rolling views of capacity, dependency and funding, so constraints are visible before they bite.

06

Benefits realisation as a governed stage

Track value after go-live against the approved business case, closing the loop between investment approval and outcome rather than declaring victory at delivery.

Informed by current enterprise portfolio-management practice.
